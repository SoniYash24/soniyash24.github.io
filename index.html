Master's Student in Communication and Signal Processing (SPCOM)
Indian Institute of Technology, Jammu

I am a Master's scholar specializing in Communication and Signal Processing (SPCOM) within the Department of Electrical Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology, Jammu. My academic journey is dedicated to exploring fundamental mathematical formulations and applied engineering paradigms in next-generation wireless communications, statistical signal processing, and intelligent information systems. This trajectory is enriched by valuable research exposures across premier national institutes, notably the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru, and Pilani, engaging in channel estimation algorithms, array processing, and communication architecture optimization.

My primary research interests center on the convergence of statistical signal analysis, massive MIMO architectures, detection and estimation theory, and the integration of machine learning techniques for adaptive physical-layer signal processing. Motivated by bridging theoretical bounds with computationally efficient real-world implementations, I frequently utilize system-level simulations, convex optimization formulations, and embedded digital signal processing analytics to resolve pressing throughput, spectral efficiency, latency, and reliability constraints in contemporary telecommunication environments.

Driven by scholarly curiosity and collaborative research, I actively author scientific publications, contribute to academic conferences, and pursue innovative engineering partnerships. I welcome collaborations with fellow researchers, academic laboratories, and forward-thinking industry teams committed to advancing 5G/6G wireless physical layers, statistical array signal processing, and high-assurance communication systems.
